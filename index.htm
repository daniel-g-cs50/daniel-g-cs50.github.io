.: Sites Related to my Lighting Hobby :.
Lamptech.co.uk - Run by a man who works in the lighting industry R n D and collects lamps. Really informative. Might have problems where the "individual lamp from collection" page comes up blank.
KiloKat's Antique Light Bulb Site - A site for antique-electric-lamp collectors. The granddaddy of them all. Near dead - no one there. (might be down)
Lighting-Gallery.net - A forum for electric lamp collectors and enthusiasts. Very active. The biggest. Created by Philip Slawinski in 2006.
Gallery of Lights - A similar site for lamp collectors and enthusiasts. Linking here in case it comes back up. It's been dead for awhile -- Coppermine Gallery software is misconfigured.
Unfortunately, allthingslighting.co.uk (known as ATL by its users) is no longer online. Dang the draconian UK regs!
